- Client's domain is registered in Client's name and is Client's property.

non-refundable once work has started. If Client cancels after the design
is approved, [50]% of the remaining balance is due. Files transfer once
amounts due are paid.

Korvane builds to current accessibility and search best practices but does
not guarantee search rankings, sales, or legal compliance (including ADA).
