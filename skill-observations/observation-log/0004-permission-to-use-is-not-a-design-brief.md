**Issue:** The owner sent a photo of a matte black Aventador SVJ as "the
Lamborghini I want you to use". Asked whether the photo was theirs, they said
yes. That answer was taken as a brief: the whole hero and scroll story were
rebuilt around the photo (street, building and all), published, and the
owner then said "Don't use that picture as the background, I just wanted only
the lambo used ... the background is just the lambo driving around". Their
first message had already said what they wanted (the car driving at night);
the photo identified the car, it was not the scene.

**Suggested improvement:** In the discovery step, when an owner supplies an
asset, record its role in one line before designing around it ("reference
for which car" vs "image to put on the page") and confirm with the owner when
the role is not stated. Re-read the owner's original scene description before
replacing it with whatever asset became available.

**Principle:** Permission to use an asset is not a decision about how to use
it. Confirm the asset's role in one line before rebuilding a design around
it, especially when it differs from the scene the person first described.
