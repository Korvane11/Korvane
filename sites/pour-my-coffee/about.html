About us

Pour My Coffee Co is a coffee shop in Rita Ranch, at the Safeway shopping center: a comfortable, relaxing place for all to enjoy.

Come in and stay a while

Enjoy one of our drinks while you catch up on work, hold a meeting, get together with friends or go on a date.

Our welcoming team will be ready to serve you.

Our coffee

Our coffee is carefully selected to be sourced from quality roasters. The beans have to meet our standards: grown by ethical farms, and always fresh.

Our food

We chose some of the best fresh ingredients for our food: fresh baked pastries, sandwiches and snacks, for breakfast, lunch or the afternoon.

Join our team

Thank you for your interest in joining our team! If you would like us to consider you, fill in the form. If you have a résumé, you can email it to PourMyCoffeeCo@gmail.com.

  • Please don't call or walk in to the coffee shop to ask about a job.
  • Because of the large number of applications, we likely won't be able to reply unless we need more information.
