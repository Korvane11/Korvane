Good to know

Before
you race.

Do I need sim racing experience?

No. New drivers can get comfortable with the controls and build pace at their own speed.

Can I race with friends?

Yes. Choose the two-, three- or six-driver session for the size of your group.

Do you take walk-ins?

Sessions are by appointment only, so book ahead online or call or text 971-345-2568.

What's included?

Every session includes SimNow's professional racing equipment: the cockpit, Fanatec direct-drive wheel and pedals, and iRacing.
